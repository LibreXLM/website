what it is

LIBRE is the main token of the Libre Trading Ecosystem — a system of many different tokens that are continuously traded against one another through liquidity pools. There is no single coin held in isolation; there is a living market of personal tokens, and LIBRE sits at its center as the hub that connects the pools.

the idea

Every trade inside the ecosystem pays a fee that stays in the pools, and over time the total number of LIBRE in circulation keeps shrinking as supply is burned. The idea is that constant trading combined with a shrinking supply should, over time, create upward pressure on price — the longer the system runs and the more it is traded, the stronger that effect should become.

This is the intention behind the design, not a promise or a guarantee. Value is meant to emerge from motion and scarcity working together — see the system explained and the live supply.
