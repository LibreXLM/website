Launching a personal token on Stellar takes minutes and costs only fractions of a cent. Here's the path from empty wallet to a token that trades inside the Libre ecosystem.

1

Get a Stellar wallet & some XLM

Install a wallet such as LOBSTR or Freighter and fund it with a small amount of XLM to cover minimum balances and network fees (a couple of XLM is plenty).

2

Create issuing & distribution accounts

Use two accounts: one issuing account that mints the token and one distribution account that holds and trades it. Keeping them separate is standard practice and keeps your supply auditable.

3

Define & issue your token

Pick a code (up to 12 characters), have the distribution account trust it, then send the amount you want to create from the issuer. That payment is the minting — your token now exists on-chain.

4

Open a liquidity pool against LIBRE

This is what plugs you into the ecosystem: create an LP pairing your token with $LIBRE (and/or XLM) and deposit both sides. Now your token can be traded continuously and starts generating fees. See how the system works →

5

Optional: lock the issuer & publish a stellar.toml

Set the issuer's master weight to zero to make the supply permanent, and publish a stellar.toml so wallets and explorers show proper metadata for your asset. This signals seriousness and builds trust.

Creating tokens is experimental and entirely your own responsibility. Only commit XLM you're comfortable spending, and remember: nothing here is financial advice.
